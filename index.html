Questions people ask

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Most clients share a door code or leave a key. We text when we arrive and when we finish.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes — supplies and equipment are included. If you prefer your own products, leave them on the counter and we will use those instead.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your slot at no charge. Inside 24 hours, we charge half the visit.

Are you insured? What about pets?

We are insured and bonded. Tell us about pets when you book so we know who will be home and which doors to keep closed.
